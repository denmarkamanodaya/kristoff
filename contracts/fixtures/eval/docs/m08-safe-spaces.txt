MUNICIPAL TRIAL COURT, FICTIONAL CITY
Criminal Case No. MTC-0008
PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINES v. MMM (accused); NNN (private complainant)

DECISION

While the complainant NNN was walking to a jeepney stop, the accused MMM followed her for
two blocks, whistled at her, and made remarks about her body. A tricycle driver who saw the
incident testified for the prosecution. The accused said he was only greeting her.

<<<PAGE BREAK>>>

RULING

Catcalling, wolf-whistling and unwanted comments on a person's appearance made in a public
place are gender-based sexual harassment in streets and public spaces. The Court finds
the testimony of the complainant and the eyewitness credible.

WHEREFORE, the accused MMM is found GUILTY under Section 11 of Republic Act No. 11313, the
Safe Spaces Act, for a first offense, and is ordered to pay a fine of One Thousand Pesos
(PHP 1,000) and to render twelve (12) hours of community service, including attendance at
a gender sensitivity seminar.

SO ORDERED.
