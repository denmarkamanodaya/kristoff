REGIONAL TRIAL COURT, BRANCH 00, FICTIONAL CITY
Civil Case No. FC-0009
OOO (plaintiff) v. PPP TRANSIT INC. and QQQ (defendants)

DECISION

The plaintiff OOO was crossing a highway at a pedestrian lane when a bus of the defendant
PPP Transit Inc., driven by the defendant QQQ, hit her. Evidence showed the bus was running
at about 90 kilometers per hour in a zone where the posted limit was 40. The plaintiff
suffered a fractured leg and lost three months of income.

<<<PAGE BREAK>>>

RULING

The driver was negligent: he exceeded the speed allowed on that road, contrary to Section
35 of Republic Act No. 4136, and failed to yield at a pedestrian lane. Under Article 2176
of the Civil Code (Republic Act No. 386), whoever by act or omission causes damage to
another through fault or negligence is obliged to pay for the damage done. The employer is
also liable under Article 2180 because it did not prove the diligence of a good father of a
family in selecting and supervising its driver.

WHEREFORE, the defendants are ordered to pay the plaintiff, jointly and severally, actual
damages of PHP 250,000, moral damages of PHP 100,000, and attorney's fees of PHP 50,000.

SO ORDERED.
