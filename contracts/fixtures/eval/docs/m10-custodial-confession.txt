PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINES v. RRR (accused)

DECISION

The accused RRR is charged with theft. The main evidence is a written confession he signed
at the police station on the night of his arrest. The investigating officer admitted that
no lawyer was present and that the accused was told he could sign first and talk to a
lawyer later.

<<<PAGE BREAK>>>

RULING

A person under custodial investigation has the right to be informed of the right to remain
silent and to have competent and independent counsel, preferably of his own choice. These
rights cannot be waived except in writing and in the presence of counsel. A confession
obtained in violation of these rights is inadmissible in evidence (1987 Constitution,
Article III, Section 12).

Without the confession, the remaining evidence does not prove guilt beyond reasonable
doubt. WHEREFORE, the accused RRR is ACQUITTED.

SO ORDERED.
