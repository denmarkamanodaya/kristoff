Criminal Case No. FC-0006
PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINES v. JJJ (accused); KKK (private complainant)

DECISION

The accused JJJ, a department manager, told his subordinate KKK that her contract would be
renewed only if she agreed to go out with him on weekends. When she refused, he gave her
an unsatisfactory rating and her contract was not renewed. Two co-workers testified that
they heard the demand.

<<<PAGE BREAK>>>

RULING

The accused, who had authority over the complainant, demanded a sexual favor as a
condition for her continued employment, and her refusal resulted in a denial of contract
renewal. These are the elements of work-related sexual harassment under Section 3 of
Republic Act No. 7877.

WHEREFORE, the accused JJJ is found GUILTY and, under Section 7 of Republic Act No. 7877,
is sentenced to imprisonment of three (3) months and to pay a fine of Ten Thousand Pesos
(PHP 10,000).

SO ORDERED.
