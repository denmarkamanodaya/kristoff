REGIONAL TRIAL COURT, BRANCH 00, FICTIONAL CITY
Criminal Case No. FC-0004
PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINES v. GGG (accused)

DECISION

The accused GGG is charged with illegal possession of dangerous drugs under Section 11 of
Republic Act No. 9165 after police officers allegedly found a sachet of methamphetamine
in his pocket during a checkpoint inspection.

The arresting officer testified that he marked the sachet only at the police station, two
hours after the arrest. No representative of the media or of the Department of Justice and
no elected public official witnessed the inventory, and the prosecution offered no
explanation for their absence.

<<<PAGE BREAK>>>

RULING

The identity of the seized drug is the corpus delicti of the offense. Section 21 of
Republic Act No. 9165 requires the physical inventory and photographing of the seized item
immediately after seizure, in the presence of the required witnesses. The unexplained
delay in marking and the absence of every required witness create reasonable doubt that
the sachet presented in court is the one taken from the accused.

WHEREFORE, the accused GGG is ACQUITTED on reasonable doubt. His immediate release is
ordered unless he is held for another lawful cause.

SO ORDERED.
