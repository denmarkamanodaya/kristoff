REGIONAL TRIAL COURT, BRANCH 00, FICTIONAL CITY
Criminal Case No. FC-0005
PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINES v. HHH (accused); minor III (offended party)

DECISION

The accused HHH, a neighbor, slapped and repeatedly shouted insults at the minor III, aged
twelve, in front of her classmates, calling her worthless. The minor stopped attending
school for two weeks. A guidance counselor testified on the emotional effect on the
child.

The accused claimed he was only disciplining the child for throwing stones at his dog.

<<<PAGE BREAK>>>

RULING

The acts of the accused debased, degraded and demeaned the intrinsic worth and dignity of
the child and were prejudicial to her development. The accused has no parental authority
over the child and the manner of the so-called discipline was excessive.

WHEREFORE, the accused HHH is found GUILTY of other acts of child abuse under Section 10(a)
of Republic Act No. 7610 and is sentenced to an indeterminate penalty of four (4) years,
nine (9) months and eleven (11) days of prision correccional, as minimum, to six (6) years,
eight (8) months and one (1) day of prision mayor, as maximum.

SO ORDERED.
