REGIONAL TRIAL COURT, BRANCH 00, FICTIONAL CITY
Criminal Case No. FC-0001
PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINES v. AAA (accused); BBB (private complainant)

DECISION

The accused AAA is charged with violation of Republic Act No. 9262, Section 5(i), for
causing mental and emotional anguish to his wife BBB. The prosecution alleged that from
January to June of the year in question, the accused repeatedly humiliated BBB in front of
their children, withheld all financial support, and sent her messages threatening to take
the children away.

BBB testified that she suffered sleeplessness and anxiety. A clinical psychologist
testified that BBB showed symptoms consistent with an anxiety disorder arising from the
marital conflict. The accused denied the acts and claimed the messages were jokes.

RULING

The Court finds that the prosecution proved every element of psychological violence under
Section 5(i) of Republic Act No. 9262: the offended party is the wife of the accused, she
suffered mental and emotional anguish, and the anguish was caused by the acts of the
accused, namely public humiliation and denial of financial support.

WHEREFORE, the accused AAA is found GUILTY beyond reasonable doubt of psychological
violence under Section 5(i) of Republic Act No. 9262. Applying Section 6 of the same Act,
he is sentenced to an indeterminate penalty of six (6) months of prision correccional, as
minimum, to eight (8) years of prision mayor, as maximum, and to pay a fine of One Hundred
Thousand Pesos (PHP 100,000). He is further ordered to undergo mandatory psychological
counseling.

SO ORDERED.
