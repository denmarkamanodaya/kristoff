SANDIGANBAYAN (FICTIONAL DIVISION)
Criminal Case No. SB-0007
PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINES v. LLL (accused), municipal mayor of Fictional Town

DECISION

The accused LLL awarded a road-repair contract worth PHP 5,000,000 to a company owned by
his brother-in-law without public bidding. The company completed only forty percent of the
work, yet the accused approved full payment. A government auditor testified on the
overpayment.

<<<PAGE BREAK>>>

RULING

The accused is a public officer who acted with manifest partiality and evident bad faith
in the discharge of his official functions, causing undue injury to the government of
about PHP 3,000,000 and giving unwarranted benefits to a private party.

WHEREFORE, the accused LLL is found GUILTY of violating Section 3(e) of Republic Act No.
3019, the Anti-Graft and Corrupt Practices Act, and is sentenced to imprisonment of six (6)
years and one (1) month to ten (10) years, with perpetual disqualification from public
office.

SO ORDERED.
