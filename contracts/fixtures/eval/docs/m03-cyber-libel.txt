REGIONAL TRIAL COURT, BRANCH 00, FICTIONAL CITY
Criminal Case No. FC-0003
PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINES v. EEE (accused); FFF (private complainant)

DECISION

The accused EEE posted on a public social media page that the private complainant FFF, a
barangay official, had stolen relief goods. The post was shared more than two thousand
times. The prosecution presented screenshots authenticated by the officer who captured
them, and evidence that no relief goods were missing.

The accused admitted writing the post but claimed it was a fair comment on a public
official.

<<<PAGE BREAK>>>

RULING

The imputation of a crime was defamatory, public, and made with malice: the accused made
no effort to verify it and refused to take it down after the audit cleared the
complainant. Fair comment does not protect a false statement of fact.

The accused is found GUILTY of libel committed through a computer system under Section
4(c)(4) of Republic Act No. 10175. Because the crime was committed through information and
communications technology, Section 6 of the same Act applies, and the penalty is one (1)
degree higher than that provided by the Revised Penal Code for ordinary libel.

SO ORDERED.
